# Content sources and licenses

Kıblegâh's code is licensed under the GPL-3.0 (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)). The religious texts it shows are **not** part of this repository and are **not** covered by that license. They belong to their publishers, and each comes with its own terms.

This repository contains no Quran text, translations, tafsir, hadith, search indexes, place lists, mushaf fonts or page layouts. The only religious text in it is the Turkish transliteration and meaning of the 99 names of Allah (`tools/content/esma_tr.json`, see the `esma` row below). Instead, `tools/content/fetch.sh` downloads every text from its original source and builds the content packs locally into `App/Resources/Paketler/` (git-ignored). Each pack carries `manifest.json` with its source (`quelle`, `quelle_url`) and license note (`lizenz`).

This page is a summary for developers, not legal advice. Read the original terms before you distribute a build, and in particular before you sell one or ship it through an app store.

## Loaded at runtime

The app fetches some content over the network when you use it. Nothing of this is stored in the repository or bundled with the app.

| Content | Source | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Recitations (verse by verse) and word-by-word audio | Quran Foundation: `verses.quran.com`, `audio.qurancdn.com` | Used under the [Quran Foundation API terms](https://api-docs.quran.foundation) (caching at most one week, attribution, revocable). |
| Word meanings and word timings | `api.quran.com` (Quran Foundation API v4), called directly by the app without OAuth | Used under the Quran Foundation API terms; permission will be requested before an App Store release. |
| Mushaf page fonts (QCF, one font per page) | KFGQPC fonts served by `verses.quran.foundation`, cached on the device for at most one week | The fonts are published by the King Fahd Glorious Quran Printing Complex; used under the Quran Foundation API terms; permission will be requested before an App Store release. They are never bundled. |
| Mushaf page layouts | Quran Foundation API through your own relay (`relay/`) | Needs your own Quran Foundation API credentials on the relay. Without a relay the mushaf view shows the reflowed text instead. |
| Some reciters | EveryAyah mirror (`mirrors.quranicaudio.com`) | The rights of the individual reciters are not documented. |

## Prayer times

Prayer times are computed on the device by our own implementation of Diyanet's method, rebuilt from the official annual tables and the published decisions: Fajr (İmsak) 18°, Isha 17° in Türkiye and 16° in Europe and North America, Asr by the Shafi'i shadow, the temkin safety margins (sunrise −7, Dhuhr +5, Asr +4, Maghrib +7 minutes) and Diyanet's rule for latitudes above 45° (2009 decision). The test suite compares the result with Diyanet's published tables for places in Türkiye, Germany and elsewhere.
